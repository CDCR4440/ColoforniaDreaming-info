# FPC and NRA Sue Pennsylvania Over Under-21 Carry Ban

**What happened.** FPC and the NRA, joined by member Daniel Maines, filed *Maines v. Bethel* in the U.S. District Court for the Eastern District of Pennsylvania. Pennsylvania bars adults under 21 from even applying for the license needed to carry concealed, carry in a vehicle, or carry at all in Philadelphia. The suit asks the court to declare that 18-to-20-year-olds have a right to carry outside the home and to block enforcement of the ban. It builds on the Third Circuit's *Lara v. Paris* holding that 18-to-20-year-olds are among "the people."

<!-- more -->

**Why it matters.** The Third Circuit already said in *Lara* that 18-to-20-year-olds are among "the people." This case tests whether Pennsylvania will honor that for licensed carry.
