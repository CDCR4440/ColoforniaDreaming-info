# 2A News

Short summaries of official releases from **Gun Owners of America (GOA)**, **Firearms Policy Coalition (FPC)**, the **Colorado State Shooting Association (CSSA)**, the **NRA Institute for Legislative Action (NRA-ILA)** and the **ATF**.

The house rule here is simple: the facts in each post come from the organization's own release, and every post links to it. The "Why it matters" line is our two cents, and we label it that way. If we got something wrong, the source link is right there to prove it.

Browse by group under **Categories**, or follow along with the [RSS feed](https://coloforniadreaming.info/feed_rss_created.xml).
