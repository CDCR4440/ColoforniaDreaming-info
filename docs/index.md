Howdy, and welcome to the reference desk of [ColoforniaDreaming.com](https://coloforniadreaming.com).

The blog and [@CDCR4440](https://x.com/CDCR4440) cover the news of the day. This wiki holds the background: dates, documents and numbers you can check, plus links to where they came from. It's written from a pro-Constitution, pro-Second Amendment, pro-free-market point of view, and we say so up front. The facts don't need a point of view, though, so every factual page ends with a **Sources** box. If a source doesn't support what we wrote, we want to hear about it.

## House rules

1. **Satire is labeled.** Anything in Myths & Legends carries a satire banner. Everywhere else, we're being serious.
2. **Facts are sourced.** Primary sources (statutes, court dockets, government data, original documents) come first. Secondary sources are marked as such.
3. **Quotes are word for word,** linked to where we found them. If we can't verify a quote, it doesn't run.
4. **Aim at ideas and policies, not private people.** Keep it family-friendly. No threats and no calls to violence. Lock keeps his horns for defense, never for conquest.
5. **Dates:** Russian events before February 1918 are given as *Old Style (New Style)*, because Russia used the Julian calendar until then.
