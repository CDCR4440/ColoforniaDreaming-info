# History

The American founding put the right to keep and bear arms in writing. Russia's revolutionary generation, from the famine of 1891–92 to the Civil War, showed what happens when a state decides the people can't be trusted with arms, bread or newspapers. This section sets those two records side by side and asks what they teach about the socialist movements active in America today.

!!! note "How we write history here"
    Every article gives dates and links its sources. Russian dates before February 1918 are written *Old Style (New Style)*. Where we offer opinion or draw a lesson for today, we label it as opinion.
