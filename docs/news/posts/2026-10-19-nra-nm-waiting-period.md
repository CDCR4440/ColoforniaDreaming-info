# NRA sues New Mexico (again) over its 7-day waiting period

**What happened.** The NRA, joined by NSSF, the Second Amendment Foundation, New Mexico gun groups and businesses, and an NRA member, filed *NSSF v. Torrez* in the U.S. District Court for the District of New Mexico. The Tenth Circuit already held New Mexico's 7-day waiting period unconstitutional in *Ortega v. Grisham*, except as to people whose background checks don't clear within three days. According to NRA-ILA, the state keeps enforcing the law against everyone except the two *Ortega* plaintiffs and has threatened criminal enforcement against retailers and buyers. The new suit seeks to stop that.

<!-- more -->

**Why it matters.** When a federal appeals court says a law is unconstitutional, the usual next step is to stop enforcing it. New Mexico's reading is that the Constitution only protects people who have already sued. That's a creative theory of rights, and the NRA is asking a court to retire it.
