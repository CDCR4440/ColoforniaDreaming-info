# Judge Won't Rubber-Stamp End of Florida's Waiting Period

**What happened.** In *Dunn v. Glass*, Judge Steven D. Merryday of the Middle District of Florida denied a joint motion asking the court to declare Florida's three-day waiting period unconstitutional. Florida's own defendants had agreed that the law violates the Second Amendment. The judge asked for a fuller presentation and invited the Governor, the Legislature, Brady and Giffords to file amicus briefs. Notices of intent are due November 30, 2026, and briefs are due February 1, 2027.

<!-- more -->

**Why it matters.** Even when the State concedes, the courts want a full record, so this fight will run into 2027.
