# Narrative vs. Reality

Each entry follows the same simple format:

1. **The Narrative:** the claim as it usually appears in headlines, ads and speeches.
2. **The Record:** what the primary data or documents actually say, with links.
3. **What's Left Out:** the context that changes the picture.
4. **Bottom Line:** one or two plain sentences.

We don't call a claim "false" if it's technically true. Many of the most effective talking points are true as worded and misleading in how they're used, and we say exactly which kind each one is.

## Entries

- **["Guns are the leading cause of death for kids"](guns-leading-cause-of-death-kids.md)**: true as the CDC words it. Look at who's counted as a "kid," and at how the deaths happen.
- **["Assault weapons are the weapon of choice for criminals"](assault-weapons-weapon-of-choice.md)**: the FBI's own homicide tables say otherwise.
