# GOA Sues Over "Enhanced" Background Checks for Under-21 Adults

**What happened.** Gun Owners of America and Gun Owners Foundation filed *Haward v. Blanche* in the Western District of Pennsylvania. The suit challenges the "enhanced" background checks for adults under 21 created by the Bipartisan Safer Communities Act. GOA argues that 18-to-20-year-olds are part of "the people" and that the checks work as age-based waiting periods. GOA also reports that the Department of Justice has appealed GOA's win against the "Engaged in the Business" rule.

<!-- more -->

**Why it matters.** A right that comes with a built-in delay for one age group of adults is being rationed. Courts should explain why 18-year-olds can serve their country but have to wait to defend themselves.
